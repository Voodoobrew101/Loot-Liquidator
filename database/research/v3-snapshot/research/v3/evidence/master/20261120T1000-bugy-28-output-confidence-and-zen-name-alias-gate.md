## Additional name-identity hold: "Zen Mystic Sapphire" is a cut-gem name
The original Bugy table labels one column **"Zen Mystic Sapphire"**, while the archive assigns a *family anchor* `192840`, the raw **Mystic Sapphire** family (`192840/192841/192842` in the main rank-family map). These names are **not interchangeable item IDs**: [Dragonflight Jewelcrafting recipes](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/professions/jewelcrafting/recipes-dragonflight) lists Zen Mystic Sapphire as a **crafted cut**; [Mystic Sapphire](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Mystic_Sapphire) describes the **uncut reagent obtained by prospecting**, and lists the Zen cut as a downstream use. An independent item-ID listing has `192905` for one Zen Mystic Sapphire crafted-quality variant ([Pawn gem listing](https://github.com/VgerMods/Pawn/blob/main/Gems.lua)). Bugy supplies no per-output item link or item quality.

**Interpretation, not an observation:** the source author may have used the craft name as a shorthand for the corresponding raw gem, but the source does not establish that equivalence or output rank. Keep the exact author label, flag `Zen Mystic Sapphire → Mystic Sapphire` as a *review-needed source-name alias*, and do not price a crafted cut as a direct Prospect result. A verified documented alias may enable source-scoped family-mean pricing later; it is not evidence of a measured rank-specific proc.
