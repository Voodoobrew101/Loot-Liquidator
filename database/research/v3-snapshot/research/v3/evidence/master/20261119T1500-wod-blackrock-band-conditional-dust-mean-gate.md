# Research-only source/units gate: 2014 WoD Blackrock Band dust/shard observations

Verified 2026-11-19 15:00 UTC. This is **an original-source conditional-mean identity check**, not a new study, a Retail-current estimate, or live-client evidence.

## Source-pinned observations and distinction

- [Scii's original Draenic Dust comment](https://www.wowhead.com/item=109693/draenic-dust) describes an earlier **10.5 Draenic Dust (109693) average per Blackrock Band ring**. A later edit reports **40 finished ring disenchant attempts yielded six Luminous Shard items (111245)** and that the average dust **when the result was not a shard** remained the same. Thus **10.5 is a historically observed conditional dust-output mean in the edited outcome regime**, not verified 10.5 unconditional dust per ring across the later 40. The earlier unsized sample may overlap. No source-supported probability of dust or individual dust quantity sum for 40 rings.
- The **only independently normalizable mean from the 40 later rings is 6 shard *items* / 40 finished disenchants = 0.15 shard units per ring**, conditioned on the unspecified ring variants and that original 2014 epoch. This is NOT a 15% occurrence probability: six items are not necessarily six positive casts; shard-positive count, zero bins, stacks, dust totals, player profession and exact input item identity are absent. No attempt to price an individual Blackrock Band input.
- [lordlight's separate original WoD comment on same page](https://www.wowhead.com/item=109693/draenic-dust) reports five rare ilvl-630 disenchants each producing **8–12 dust**; all five are positive but there is no five-stack histogram, total quantity or identified gear item. The minimum/maximum cannot justify an EV midpoint.
- [Blizzard November 25 2014 hotfix archive](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/16561638), corroborated by the [Warcraft Wiki historic archive](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Hotfixes/2014_Archive), states a change in rare-item dust-vs-shard likelihood and increased dust for Enchanters, **without any numeric coefficient**. Do not transpose this historic patch-state into present Retail or mix data across the hotfix without collection dates.
