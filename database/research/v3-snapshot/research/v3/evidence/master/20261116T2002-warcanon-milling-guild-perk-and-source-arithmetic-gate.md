## Guild-perk scope is a negative control, not a 15% milling multiplier

The original warcanon comment merely says **"this was done with the guild perk"**, without identifying a profession output modifier. The historical **Bountiful Bags** perk [description and patch history](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Bountiful_Bags) and the [2011-era spell and comments](https://www.wowhead.com/cata/spell=83966/bountiful-bags) explicitly scope its extra material reward to **Mining, Skinning, Herbalism and Disenchanting**; it does *not* list Milling or Prospecting. [A contemporary explanation](https://www.engadget.com/2010-07-12-officers-quarters-rating-cataclysms-guild-perks-part-2.html) likewise identifies those two processing professions as excluded. Bountiful Bags could have affected *herbs gathered* before processing, but the author's separately measured number of herbs consumed and milling output bins do not establish any 15% output bonus, input refund or probability coefficient. Moreover, the wiki records removal from Retail in patch **6.0.2 (2014-10-14)**. Preserve the historical "guild perk active, unspecified" context and **apply no guild multiplier to expected milling outputs**. Do not infer the original author necessarily meant Bountiful Bags without a more explicit source.
