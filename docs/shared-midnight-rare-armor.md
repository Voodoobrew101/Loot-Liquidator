The owner-submitted complete disenchant observations now form the built-in
`gear:11:3:armor` reference. Matching uses expansion, rarity and armor class,
not an item number, armor subtype or the local-recordings switch.

The importer now handles every usable captured gear category rather than only
Midnight rare armor. The shared groups contain 544 Midnight rare-armor casts,
three rare-weapon casts, 17 epic-armor casts and six epic-weapon casts. Existing
reviewed sources add nine Midnight epic-armor casts, one epic-weapon cast,
66 Dragonflight epic-armor casts and 17 epic-weapon casts: 663 distinct promoted
receipts across six physical categories. The known-profile retained cohorts
keep their profile gates and are checked against overlapping personal records.

The owner explicitly approved treating these archived uploads as the standard
observed gear-category reference. This is a fixed empirical reference under the
conditions represented in those recordings. The 570 older complete receipts
retain their mixed and sometimes unknown skill context in provenance; they do
not establish a result for every skill, specialization or later client build.
The other 93 retained receipts keep their existing measured-profile and build
restrictions. The total of 663 counts distinct receipts, not one compatible
663-cast study.

This approval does not change intake policy for new community submissions.
Their complete, independently reviewed disenchant cohorts pool only when gear
category, measured profession profile and client build match. The separate
issue #94 review contributes 27 new disenchants in 26 whole cohorts, compiled
into 22 such records; it does not merge those operations into the older mixed
owner reference or treat its whole 2,000-record history as new evidence.

The full intake audit also covers the original two-page November 6 export and
the compact November 22 export. Older/green and other source observations with
unresolved recording coverage or unverified Timewalking conditions remain in
the research archive without inventing measured normal-era drop rates.

The reference contains 544 distinct complete casts: 500 rank-1 and 44 rank-2
Radiant Shards. These include all 405 usable skill-82 casts, 112 skill-64 casts
and 27 complete casts with unreported effective skill. Original measured
contexts are retained in the source archives and profile totals in the compiled
record. Cumulative exports, including the 97/194 snapshots, count once. The
owner-reviewed twenty-cast exception retains its hash-bound denominator review.

The importer uses the existing evidence compiler and confidence calculation.
These are observed category rates, not measurements at zero skill and not a
new inferred coefficient for skill, equipment or specialization. The measured
category reference takes precedence over older synthetic category scenarios;
it is not run through those zero-reference projections. Output-access gates
still run. These captures have no invested specialization. Explicit what-if
profiles and profiles with invested specialization continue to use the existing
simulation curves rather than asserting that these casts measured those
allocations. Source-profile precision and applicability to
an unmeasured profile are distinct uncertainties.

Confidence calculated from the observed counts measures support for those
recorded quantities. A high score does not verify applicability to an
unmeasured profile or a different client build. The shared reference does not
claim a calibrated skill response or a new current-build experiment.

Exports with unresolved disenchant coverage remain preserved as research.
In particular the 104-skill batch has 200 confirmed results but only 199
classified receipts. Its missing material is not invented or silently counted
as zero, and dividing by 199 is not presented as its complete-batch rate.
This limitation does not prevent the complete 544-cast category reference
from supporting estimates in the built-in database.

Tooltip method confidence now follows the valued model. Missing chance/range
display fields keep their own uncertainty but cannot force that whole header
to zero. A model whose actual value confidence is zero still displays zero.

Regression coverage checks the complete compiled corpus, arbitrary matching
item IDs, personal recordings disabled, rarity/expansion/weapon boundaries,
exact unit totals and valuation, raw-source immutability, and tooltip fields.
