## The actual Claude–ChatGPT agreement

PDF pp. 17–18 explicitly names the live browser conversation and agreed threads S5–S12. These are present in `research/v3/strategy.md` at the reviewed research head. They permit labeled provisional estimates, separate ranks/patches/unlocks, count each original study once, base occurrence confidence on occurrence measurements, preserve observed ranges, and plan separate recommendation confidence. This is the requested discussion; access to arbitrary private conversation history was unnecessary to locate the recorded agreement.

Three qualifications matter when applying the draft statistical examples:

- “50 prospects, 12 Alicite” (p. 13) establishes 12 hit events only if the source means 12 operations with that output, or a one-unit conditional yield is independently established. Twelve material units can come from fewer hits. Preserve totals and hit counts as different measurements.
- Midpoint-derived means/chances and an initial 1–1 range (pp. 13–14) are assumptions. The agreed S5 policy and current charter allow defensible provisional use with labeling; these are never measured distributions, source-observed bounds or invented samples.
- The community Resourcefulness and Multicraft formulas (pp. 5–6) are proposals with applicability limits. They do not prove recipe/patch-specific coefficients or justify applying unverified Multicraft. A nominal-input study with refunds cannot silently become a counted-operation study. Later owner baseline/scaling clarification and the current charter preserve these distinctions.
