## Crucial false-positive prevention
The [Writhebark R2 item page](https://www.wowhead.com/item=191471/writhebark) displays a **17.19% “Dropped by Kangalo”** statistic, which is a creature loot rate, NOT milling P(hit). Likewise some Flourishing pigment pages have creature-drop information; never import those percentages into milling. These pages do not expose a usable Wowhead Milling/Prospected-from per-cast histogram for the assigned tuples.

## Economic invariants and priority
Pinned TSM source rows all have `matRate=1`, `amountOfMats=0.7`, `minAmount=2`, `maxAmount=5`, repeated across three possible pigment output quality ranks. These are historical family/rank alternatives with **unverified rate units**; they are NOT 3 simultaneously guaranteed outputs. Blindly summing `0.7*3=2.1` for one herb rank would risk a triple-count, but no current Loot Liquidator production error is established. Never convert gross herbs / 5 into verified casts when Resourcefulness may return herbs; saved herbs affect input cost, not pigment output. All exact current economic P/E and tooltip-only P/min/max remain unknown.
