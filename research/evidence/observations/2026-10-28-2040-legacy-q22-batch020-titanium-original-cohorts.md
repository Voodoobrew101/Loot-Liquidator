## NEW original author: kuraiummei, 2009 Wrath 3.2 PTR
- Source: https://www.wowhead.com/item=36910/titanium-ore; author kuraiummei; secondary indexed timestamp June 27, 2009 (not independently validated by the original page).
- Original stated **720 Titanium Ore / 144 completed prospects**, with six common gem totals: Huge Citrine 41, Chalcedony 31, Bloodstone 43, Dark Jade 39, Shadow Crystal 33, Sun Crystal 27.
- Original stated **94 Titanium Powder** and **12 operations with two powder**. With no larger-than-two event mentioned, the zero-inclusive inferred powder histogram is **0:62, 1:70, 2:12**, since 12×2 +70=94 and 144−12−70=62. This is a reconstruction conditional on two being the maximum, not a fully logged cast histogram.
- Historical per-completed-prospect powder E=94/144=0.65277778, P(hit)=(70+12)/144=0.56944444, observed positive min=1, max=2. Confidence 55/100 (old PTR, original comment accessible via search index, inferred zero count and skill unrecorded). **None are current Retail 12.1 fields**.
- Six historical per-cast gem means: 36917: 43/144=0.29861111; 36920: 27/144=0.18750000; 36923: 31/144=0.21527778; 36926: 33/144=0.22916667; 36929: 41/144=0.28472222; 36932: 39/144=0.27083333. These are actual 144 reported operations, not ore/5 proxy. Per-gem occurrence probabilities and positive yield bounds were not reported and remain unknown; TSM source bounds are separate low-confidence references.

## Safety and research implications
- A valid old PTR four-field tuple is not a valid 2026 current-Retail baseline. Preserve separate patch/source contexts.
- Never assume a gem's total/144 equals its P(hit); multiple gems may be awarded in one operation.
- No source proves 2026 client skill, cast count, or AH price. Do not assert a gold-optimal action flip.
