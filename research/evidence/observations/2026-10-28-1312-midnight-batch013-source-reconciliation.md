## Research and valuation consequences
Gemdust is a product of crushing, not prospecting. The required stone is an input cost, not a bonus output. The two Gemdust quality IDs 242620 and 242621 are alternatives whose occurrence distribution depends on unknown conditions. Do not value both as guaranteed simultaneously. No new exact input/recipe/output mapping is independently proven by this run; the 16 existing input IDs remain candidate-level pending in-client per-ID eligibility.
