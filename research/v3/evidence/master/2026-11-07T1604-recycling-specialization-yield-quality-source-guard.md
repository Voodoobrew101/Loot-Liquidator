## Original and secondary source statements

- [Wow-professions Midnight Engineering guide](https://www.wow-professions.com/midnight/engineering-guide), retrieved 2026-11-07, says a recycle consumes **5 eligible crafted reagent items**; output is **around 3** Evercore or Aetherlume *at 30 points in Recycling*, and describes **doubling base output**. Those are guide/reference approximations, not a raw logged histogram or proven universal factor. Guide separately says output quality depends on crafting skill and reagent quality.
- [Boostmatch Midnight Engineering guide](https://boostmatch.gg/blog/wow/articles/wow-midnight-engineering-guide), retrieved 2026-11-07, independently presents an *approximate base reference* **~1.5 outputs/recycle** and a **~3 outputs/recycle at 30 Recycling KP**. Same units: output-family items per five-input Recycling operation. Do not present those rounded estimates as cast-recorded means.
- [Wow-professions specialization guide](https://www.wow-professions.com/midnight/engineering-specialization-guide-and-builds) describes **30 points in Recycling as a substantial skill bonus** and recommends 30 Recycling plus 10 Bits and Bots for high-quality recycled reagents. Its focus is output rank and skill, not a quantified observed yield increase. This does not by itself verify the leveling guide's claim of a literal, invariant 2.0 output-unit coefficient.
- [Method Engineering guide](https://www.method.gg/guides/midnight-engineering-profession-guide) explicitly separates **input quality affects output quality**, not quantity, and describes Resourcefulness-related specializations. Treat recovered input units/refunds separately from the number of output-family items obtained. Guidance on Multicraft and Recycling applicability varies; retain the owner's **Multicraft OFF unless independently verified for this exact processing spell**.
- The [March 21 Draggoth player example](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/impossible-patron-orders/2276246/16), already archived in [the 13:07 coordinator note](2026-11-07T1307-powder-evercore-conditional-reference.md), says **1.3 Evercore-family units per five Powder Pigment at 10 Recycling KP**. Different KP and unknown patch/quality/tool/skill conditions: never multiply that reported illustrative 1.3 by 2 to assert a measured 30-KP result.
- The [April 25 firsthand report](https://www.reddit.com/r/woweconomy/comments/1sv5u5a/recycling_changes/) says rank-1 Powder Pigment used to give rank-2 Evercore, but yielded rank-1 after an update; the reporter suspected lost skill. This is a **player-observed rank/skill eligibility difference**, not evidence of changed output count or an official patch coefficient. Do not transform rank migration into quantity scaling.
