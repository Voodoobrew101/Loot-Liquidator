## Scoped disposition and precise model separation
- The guide plus independent player comments support **an extra-gem benefit tied to a Prospecting Pro final-node state**. They do **not** provide the rate, conditional yield distribution, input saved, gem occurrence probability, rarity-by-rank mapping or specific affected outputs. Mechanics registry M16 currently bundles this qualitative effect with a broader skill/quality claim and marks the composite *unverified*; preserve uncertainty for the broader skill claim. Avoid upgrading the composite claim to a verified numeric formula.
- **Critical field distinction:** an additional gem *when a gem drops* changes the conditional quantity distribution/expected units, not necessarily **P(at least one gem)**. This is not a Multicraft proc, does not imply extra Stone/Glass, and does not justify a global x2 gem output coefficient. Treat capstone unlocked/not unlocked/unknown as **separate observational conditions**; do not blend incompatible data or create synthetic per-cast counts.
