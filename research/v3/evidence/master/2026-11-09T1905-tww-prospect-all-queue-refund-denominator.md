## Numeric-unit boundary relevant to T0008/T0040

Distinguish **initial stock** (1,000 ore), **nominal queued actions** (200 at five ore/action in this stated scenario), **completed successes** (not counted by the post), **gross ore consumption**, **Resourcefulness-refunded inputs**, and **net ore consumed**. Refund events do not themselves imply additional output-processing actions.
