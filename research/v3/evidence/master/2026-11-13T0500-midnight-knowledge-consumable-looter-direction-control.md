# T0049 source-eligibility control: Glimmering Powder is an output/consumable, not proven disenchant input

Reviewed 2026-11-13 UTC. **Research-only identity/context check; zero new successful casts, material units, chance distributions, or Retail gold valuations.** Worker originals are unchanged.

## Verified source facts and lineage

1. [Current WoWhead Glimmering Powder 267653](https://www.wowhead.com/item=267653/glimmering-powder) identifies the item as an **item-level-1, bind-on-pickup, use-to-study consumable** granting one Midnight Enchanting Knowledge, with **Cannot be destroyed** displayed. Its own **Disenchanting (2)** panel nevertheless says **"This item has been disenchanted 8 times"** and displays two **Radiant Shard** source rows, **5 out of 8 (63%)** and **3 out of 8 (38%)**, with no independently linked player event/stack quantity/build or quality-rank proof. These are **site-attribution counts**, not demonstrated processing of consumable 267653. Worker already captured both in [2026-11-13T0413-looter-identity-anomaly.jsonl](../midnight/20261113T0413-looter-identity-anomaly.jsonl); these numbers are **not new records**.
2. [WoWhead Shimmering Dust 227662](https://www.wowhead.com/item=227662/shimmering-dust), the analogous **TWW knowledge-point consumable**, also displays a source-side **"This item has been disenchanted 10 times"** table and four shard/crystal output rows (**6/10**, **2/10**, **1/10**, **1/10**). This is an **independent cross-expansion negative-control for item-page table interpretation**, not proof that either consumable could be disenchanted, not a new legitimate conversion study, and **not evidence that the 267653/250012 records are duplicate operations**.
3. The [Midnight Disenchant spell 1280952](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1280952/disenchant) specifies a target of **good-quality (green) or better armor or weapons**, destroyed in the process. Item 267653's documented use is knowledge consumption, not eligible armor/weapon processing. The [Midnight Enchanting guide](https://www.wow-professions.com/midnight/enchanting-guide) instead describes **Glimmering Powder as a conditional product of disenchanting eligible gear after weekly knowledge requirements**. Thus input item 267653 and output item 267653 are **different graph roles**: the source-side Looter table is insufficient to reverse the processing edge.
4. [Earlier source evidence from krathalan](../midnight/2026-10-31T0411-prospect-de-catchup-and-exclusions.jsonl) already records **45 Glimmering Powders from 145 reported item disenchants** (record `midnight-20261031-13002`), and [condition record `midnight-20261031-13003`](../midnight/2026-10-31T0411-prospect-de-catchup-and-exclusions.jsonl) captures weekly catch-up prerequisites. This is **already archived, not a new cohort**, lacks per-rarity denominators/complete eligibility context and is **knowledge output, not a tradable material gold yield**. [Live item comments](https://www.wowhead.com/item=267653/glimmering-powder) and [the specialist guide](https://www.wow-professions.com/midnight/enchanting-guide) present conditions but not a general unconditional gold coefficient.
5. **Important guard:** `Cannot be destroyed` text **alone** is not a universal disenchant predicate. [Glimmer of Order 201360](https://www.wowhead.com/item=201360/glimmer-of-order) likewise says `Cannot be destroyed` yet explicitly instructs an Enchanter to **disenchant it for profession knowledge**. Require the actual item kind, user action or known exception, and spell eligibility; do not infer an unconditional ban on all special knowledge items from the text alone.
