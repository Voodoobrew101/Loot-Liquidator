## Verified source statements and separable identities

1. [Original Retail Wowhead Runecloth Headband comments](https://www.wowhead.com/item=13866/runecloth-headband): **vikn** reports disenchanting **400 Runecloth Headbands (13866)**, with **source-reported unconditional averages per disenchanted item**: **3.5 Rich Illusion Dust (156930)**, **0.04 Large Brilliant Shard (14344)**, **0.6 Greater Eternal Essence (16203)**. Those numbers are *means* rather than per-output hit probabilities or raw total/histogram receipts. Author's sample date, build, item-link bonuses, Enchanting skill and profession context are not stated. Preserved already as `legacy-20261101-411..413` in `research/v3/evidence/legacy/2026-11-01T0935-outland-disenchant-and-runecloth-originals.jsonl`; do not recapture or recount as new observations.
2. The independent **urbanmonkey83** comment already archived there reports **43 separate disenchants**, **14 Greater Eternal Essences** and **58 unspecified "dust" units**. This is *not* independent corroboration of **156930 Rich** versus **16204 Light**; the dust material ID remains **null**. Existing source `legacy-20261101-414..415` retains the unknown identity; do not promote 58 to either dust or combine 43 with 400 as one profile-coherent cohort.
3. [Retail item reference](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Runecloth_Headband) shows the current catalog identity **Runecloth Headband 13866, item level 29, uncommon cloth head**, made from 6 Bolts of Runecloth and 2 Rune Thread. This is **catalog context**, not a verified 2026 character-owned item-link/bonus or successful disenchant.
4. On the [Rich Illusion Dust Retail WoWhead entry (156930)](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=156930/rich-illusion-dust) another commenter, **Zulatomten**, describes the **Shadowlands prepatch** rule that characters above level 25 disenchanting gear item level **29 and above** produce Outland/Northrend rather than Classic materials; **Valkyl** similarly notes older level-squish outcome changes. This directly meets the reference item's **level-29 boundary**, but is **historical testimony, not a confirmed rule for current Midnight Retail**, nor proof that vikn's 400-cast data was collected under the same conditions. Treat this as a *version/character eligibility hold*, not as universally false or an always-blocked action. Classic-version pages showing level 59 are not Retail evidence.
5. **Rich and Light dust are different priced items**, not output-quality ranks of one item. [Rich Illusion Dust](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Rich_Illusion_Dust) / [Retail TSM item 156930](https://tradeskillmaster.com/retail/us/items/156930) explicitly have a one-item use transformation **1 Rich (156930) → 3 Light (16204)**; [Wowhead spell 262120](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=262120/light-illusion-dust) confirms the output quantity. [Light Illusion Dust](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Light_Illusion_Dust) / [Retail Wowhead item 16204](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/item=16204/light-illusion-dust) provide the converse **3 Light → 1 Rich**, also detailed by [spell 262119](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=262119/rich-illusion-dust). The reversible conversion is **an optional, separate item-use pathway**, not an additional result of disenchanting and not proof of current-character eligibility or zero transaction costs. It must never be counted in addition to both starting and converted dust as parallel disenchant outputs.

## Gold-decision and supported-field implication

The 400-cast source supports **E[Rich 156930 units per DE] = 3.5** as an undated **historical mean**, independently of unknown hit probability and range. The source's 0.04 Shard and 0.6 Essence entries likewise remain *unconditional means*, **not 4%/60% occurrences**. With hypothetical eligible dust-conversion and defined net unit prices, *one* 3.5-Rich estimate could be quoted at **3.5 × max(P\_Rich, 3 × P\_Light)**, *not* **3.5 P\_Rich + 10.5 P\_Light**. This is a **decision-rule illustration only**; no prices, market fees, input/processing costs, conversion abilities or live characters were evaluated here. A real engine must validate eligibility and cost conditions first. The unresolved 58 dust units from the 43-cast source cannot price either material without identity.

The already committed [integration baseline probe](../../../../docs/usable-data-audit/baseline-probes.json) has **Disenchant:13866:x1** with **410.86 copper per input at its fixed synthetic prices** and labels eligibility **"Not tested; source audit only"**. This proves only the *offline baseline example exists*, not present live viability; the protected three-pass branch and immutable `4df9ae8` snapshot were **not** rebuilt or re-evaluated in this audit.

**Disposition:** historical expected quantities are **usable but version-scoped**, whereas **modern material identity and character applicability are unverified**. The missing condition is not missing TSM prices. If selected, show provenance/epoch uncertainty and leave hit/range unknown, never silently substitute Light for Rich or count the reversible dust conversion twice.
