## New source-direction finding

The Legacy worker's [original 2.4.3 group reference](https://legacyofwarcraft.com/db/tbc/professions/disenchanting.html) explicitly reports **uncommon armor, item level 102–120**: 75% Arcane Dust 22445, positive 2–5; 22% Greater Planar Essence 22446, positive 1–2; 3% Large Prismatic Shard 22449, one. Its companion **weapon/shield/off-hand** 102–120 group swaps dust/essence 75% / 22%. These are **source-model per-result percentages and source-listed conditional quantity bounds**; they are not observed counts, sampled probabilities or proven 2026 Retail mechanics. Group item-catalog counts (armor 555, weapons 205) are **items**, not operations.

An independently opened item-specific [**Wrath 3.3.5** database listing for Warmaul Legplates (24995)](https://wotlk.evowow.com/?item=24995) confirms base **item level 108**, labeled disenchantable (275), and three disenchant model *weights*: Arcane Dust **7500**, Greater Planar Essence **2200**, Large Prismatic Shard **300**. The integer weights normalize to the same **75% / 22% / 3%** as the 2.4.3 *armor* table, supporting that direction **for a historical item record**. The 3.3.5 listing is a separate game-epoch catalog and is **not independent player casts or proof of 2.4.3 engine behavior**.

However, multiple archived **WoWWiki** armor item pages in the same historical item-level range display the **reverse** result assignment: [Conqueror's Legplates, 114](https://wowwiki-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Conqueror%27s_Legplates), [Runed Sketh'lon Legplates, 111](https://wowwiki-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Runed_Sketh%27lon_Legplates), and [Warlord's Iron-Legplates, 120](https://wowwiki-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Warlord%27s_Iron-Legplates) give 22% dust / 75% essence / 3% shard. The generic [TBC Classic disenchanting reference](https://www.wow-professions.com/tbc/disenchanting-tables) and [Warcraft Wiki disenchanting mechanics](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Disenchanting) instead state that uncommon armor more commonly yields dust and weapons more commonly yield essence. The precise cause of the per-item WoWWiki inversion (e.g. historical template error, changed data, source scope) is **unresolved**. **Do not automatically overwrite** one source with another or treat copied generic percentages as extra measured evidence.

## Current-Retail eligibility is a different question

The [LIVE WoWDB item 24995](https://www.wowdb.com/items/24995-warmaul-legplates) is level **31** and explicitly says **“Cannot be disenchanted”**. This is a present catalog flag, **not a firsthand observed 2026 in-game rejection**. The Wrath item 108 and LIVE catalog item 31 are different era contexts; the 2.4.3 model must not enter a current Retail action without an exact current itemLink/bonus/ilvl, skill and client-build eligibility check. Current Wowhead listings also have alternate scaled item versions. An archived source giving different eligibility is not necessarily false if its game epoch differs.

**Boundary guard:** The model table's item-level coverage is **102–120** (no documented 100–101). Some older guides say **100–120**. No observation or source mapping in this check justifies filling or stretching levels 100/101; retain the original band and game flavour.
