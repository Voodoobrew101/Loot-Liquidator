## New source-language identity reconciliation

The original [Kaliope Jan 2007 blog and numbered replies](https://kaliope.wordpress.com/2007/01/05/adamantite-prospecting-stats/) matter more than a generic modern stack-size assumption. Comment **17** (Ture, 2007-02-02) expressly explains that comment **15**'s first ore **stack** meant **four prospects** and **20 Adamantite ores**, and in the same reply Ture reports **three blue gems on a stack**. Accordingly each 20-ore stack represents four *source-described nominal five-ore prospects*; it is **not** a captured operation-level ledger or evidence that the blue units occurred on independent casts. Worker records [legacy-20261115-0746-001, -002] currently keep `casts:null`, correctly preserving unknown directly observed operation events.

These are **raw rare-quality aggregate quantity ratios**, *not* probabilities, per-gem means, measured individual output IDs, complete sample-level output histograms or estimates of current Retail drops. In particular two blue gems in a stack do not imply two hit casts, and a zero count for specific gems cannot be assigned. The source does not supply output identity among Talasite/Living Ruby/etc. The batch-normalized ratios cannot be individually priced without a defensible sourced material-family alternative pricing policy and period/context applicability.

**Patch split is mandatory.** [2.1.0 notes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_2.1.0) place the always-at-least-one-gem change on **2007-05-22**. Comment **23** (Kaliope, 2007-06-13) reports a separate *typical*, non-experimental after-patch impression of around **one blue per 20 ore**, i.e. a *reference* **0.25 blue per nominal prospect**; comment **20** (May 7) reports a pre-patch subjective one per two stacks (**0.125 per nominal prospect**). Neither reference is an additional measured study, nor should they be averaged with the three raw aggregate totals. [Wowhead commenter 15892's](https://www.wowhead.com/wotlk/item=23425/adamantite-ore) **16.2%** is another unmeasured author claim, not an observed denominator or interchangeable with these units.
