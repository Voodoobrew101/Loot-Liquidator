## Original statements, preserved without conversion into samples

One firsthand commenter compares approximate **rank-3 Storm Dust units per real-world hour** from a crafted-green disenchant workflow (~1,200/hour) and a crafted-blue workflow (~390/hour), and says a Blood Elf performed the disenchanting. In a June follow-up the commenter says tailored hats were used for greens; they were *assuming* other crafted greens have similar yields, not reporting tests of all such items. The original poster discusses blue gear, disenchanting and subsequent shard-to-dust processing. Another commenter suggests Thaumaturgy. These workflows may include different conversion operations and process times.

These are **per-hour workflow throughput claims**, not per-input means, per-disenchant means, P(hit), output ranges, observed disenchants, material-output histograms or proof of a racial/skill coefficient. They cannot be divided by a guessed crafts-per-hour figure, assigned to arbitrary gear IDs, treated as independent green/blue sample counts, or priced as current per-action EV. The numeric figures are explicitly presented as approximate user claims, **not validated measured casts**.

## Required identity and unit fields before numeric admission

- Exact input gear itemID/itemLink, bonus/scaled item level, quality/slot, quantity and crafting cost; separate green and blue input populations.
- Operation chain: direct disenchant versus blue-to-shard followed by shattering and/or thaumaturgy; complete ingredient/output IDs and ranks for each step.
- Completed disenchants and downstream conversions, output totals by material/quality, zero-event bins or a defensible original report of means **per actual input/operation**, refunds/reprocessing, and session time.
- Player profession skill, specialization/unlocks, Blood Elf racial context, client build and study date.

**REVIEWED; CONTEXT-ONLY HOLD.** Useful for identifying a potentially significant gold/time tradeoff and a profile distinction (Blood Elf versus unspecified), but adds **zero** estimator-eligible Retail yield records, **zero** empirically supported per-operation output means/probabilities, and **zero** new supported gold decisions. Do not confuse hourly material throughput with per-disenchant output units. Preserve as a single independent original source, and do not duplicate Codex's completed 731-source catalogue.
