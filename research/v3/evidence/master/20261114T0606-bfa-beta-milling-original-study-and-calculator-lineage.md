# Coordinator original-source audit: 2018 BfA milling, 630,000 herbs (beta environment)
 
**Source:** BilisOnyxia, original first-person Reddit post and clarifying comments, [BfA Milling Data (30k Anchor Weed, 600k common herbs)](https://www.reddit.com/r/woweconomy/comments/98oyfb/bfa_milling_data_30k_anchor_weed_600k_common_herbs/), published in 2018 (exact date not independently recovered). This audit is a *single original author study* spanning seven inputs, **not seven independent studies or 21 independently sampled cohorts**. Its aggregate outcomes have not been imported into ItemDB/estimates by this coordinator. No live 2026 character, prices, automated estimator run, or add-on tooltip check.

## Version, applicability, and methodological gates

1. **This is a BfA-era 2018 beta-server dataset, not verified modern Retail.** In a first-person reply, BilisOnyxia says they repeatedly copied a live character to the beta realm to create the large batches. The author says a *smaller live-server check* agreed but publishes no quantitative live denominator/totals. Do not reclassify 630,000 beta herb inputs or 36,000 beta operations as live-Retail client captures; patch and exact collection dates, skill, talents and output quality are unstated.
2. **Anchor Weed Mass Mill was explicitly reported bugged** (~40% lower Viridescent quantity than 5-herb manual). This study deliberately used **manual** Anchor Weed. Never transfer its Anchor Weed expected units to Mass Mill without an era- and method-matched fix record. For common herbs, the author reports qualitatively similar manual/mass results in a small check; no separate numerical paired source is supplied.
3. No per-material castsWithDrop, zero-inclusive 0…N histograms, min/max observed within an operation, output rank, source build, item-link bonuses, or contemporaneous AH prices. **Leave chance, positive-hit average, range, skill adjustment, modern eligibility and price confidence unknown**. One author's seven inputs share method/era, not seven independent confirmations.
4. The [Canadia_TV 'Milling herbs BFA' calculator](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Kx2qPiGXGqS5_zla1sSN69yxrYlGdEVTzR4bypP8yJU/edit) was independently read by the coordinator using its native cell formulas: Crimson estimated quantity = `B3*31%`, Viridescent estimated quantity = `B3*13%`; B3 is *herb inputs* (example 1,000), not completed casts. The sheet does **not identify a herb**. This original BilisOnyxia six-common-herb source has ~31.55% Crimson and ~13.21% Viridescent units/herb, a **similar but not identical rate**. The historical [Wowhead Economy Weekly Wrap-Up article](https://www.wowhead.com/news/wow-economy-weekly-wrap-up-8-1-5-final-prep-effective-token-usage-milling-290611) links original mass milling research, but similarity and citation do not prove the Canadia sheet is the same original study or supply a legitimate input ID to its two held generic calculator rows. **Do not overwrite or double count either origin.**
5. BfA's 2019 Star Moss batch and 2020 Winter's Kiss ~90k source already in `research/v3/evidence/legacy/2026-11-06T1453-bfa-pigment-averages-and-tbc-discharge-eligibility.jsonl` are **different authors, dates and cohorts**; do not merge their per-input averages or treat later hotfix epochs as exchangeable. Other research snapshots might contain the same Bilis original with different names; require stable original-study ID `reddit-98oyfb-bilisonxyia-bfa-2018-beta` (note spelling alias corrected below) and deduplication review before compiler admission.

**Proposed stable lineage key (exact):** `reddit-98oyfb-bilisonyxia-bfa-2018-beta` (one original author dataset, seven herb partitions). Prior paragraph's example string is an accidental alternate spelling and must **not** be a second ID.

## Disposition / next supported implementation step

**Class:** original-source historical *beta* aggregate with verified item IDs, unambiguous input/output quantities and batch-size separation; **potentially usable with explicit BfA beta/reference epoch, not admitted to current Retail gold decisions.** The existing protected three-pass integration branch already distinguishes reference-only sources from current-compatible quantities; this audit supplies a new source lineage for an independent, reviewable compatibility test, not an instruction to overwrite that branch. Where the current engine cannot express source epoch+mass-mill recipe separately, hold rather than quietly convert values to modern five-herb Mill. Highest immediate player-relevant gap remains rank-specific, zero-inclusive Midnight T0005/T0039/T0045 operation receipts.
