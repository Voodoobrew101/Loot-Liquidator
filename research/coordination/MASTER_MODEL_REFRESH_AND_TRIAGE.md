# Master-owned research model refresh, mechanics refinement and active-learning queue

**Status:** Research-only coordination protocol, updated 2026-10-28 for user instructions. Do not modify production `main` or release the addon. Existing hourly schedules remain unchanged. Master owns the only research-candidate compilation and canonical registry/assignment promotion; four expansion workers own independent immutable evidence and worker submissions.

## Continuous master cycle: CLOSE THE LOOP

1. **INGEST and independently QA:** Read live master queue, raw immutable study observations, source-memory, worker submissions, prior candidate manifest, latest research mechanics registry, `research/evidence/observation.schema.json`, historical-mechanics chronology and any reviewed contextual temporal attributions. Validate actual cast denominator, item and recipe identity, output ranks, source ancestry and source-of-dates; do not discard samples for missing profession stats. Accept/reject worker claims explicitly and track corrections separately.
2. **RESOLVE HISTORICAL CONTEXT:** Classify each sample against applicable historical patch/bonus rules as of its observed gameplay date, not the date it was retrieved or the current game version. Study publication is a time bound only. Read original wording/logs/linked commentary to propose `CONTEXTUALLY_BEFORE/AFTER` with auditable evidence and alternatives when actual cast dates are absent; require independent QA before using contextual placement as a firm analysis stratum. The mechanics proposal currently lives in open PR #3 (`review/profession-mechanics-registry-v0.2`), and must be reconciled into `research/processing-confidence` only through a reviewed integration. Do NOT edit the open PR and canonical registry as two competing authorities.
3. **REFINE MECHANICS WHEN JUSTIFIED:** Produce a time-scoped mechanics-rule revision when evidence genuinely changes mechanic eligibility, patch boundary, effect target, parameter or applicability. Preserve historical rules/source observations and append source-backed revision lineage, effective dates, affected IDs and confidence. Qualitative evidence can refine rule existence/timing, but cannot automatically become a numeric coefficient; the master's single-writer canonical registry can promote a reviewed, tested rule only. Do not rewrite a registry for no substantive change.
4. **REPLAY AND REBUILD CANDIDATES:** Reprocess all relevant immutable cohorts against the latest **reviewed** registry and chronology. Maintain exactly one candidate unskilled (minimum recipe-eligible skill, zero optional bonus) baseline per legitimate item/output/recipe/rank/target patch. Re-evaluate the four separate cells `dropChance`, `yieldMin`, `yieldMax`, `expectedQuantity` with field-specific provenance and 0–100 confidence; preserve null for unsupported fields and use below-90 provisional model estimates where defensible. Model skilled observations by inverse rule only when appropriate/identifiable; forward projection is the mechanics layer's job. Respect additive effects and actual unlocks (zero cannot be multiplied), output-rank distribution, patch boundaries and source unit semantics. Preserve historical observational contexts outside the shipped database.
5. **GENERATE DETERMINISTIC VERSIONED ARTIFACTS:** Master alone owns model-candidate build outputs in `research/proposed/` and reviews lossless coverage versus legacy `main/GameData.lua` and overlays. Rebuild when source/registry changes, preserve source hashes, registry and chronology revision, model version, reasons and exact field changes, and rerun static invariants/regression tests. *If the itemdb compiler is still missing, clearly mark the build as `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`, and generate only honest reviewable candidate/triage artifacts; never claim `itemdb.lua` was generated or deployed.* Do not write `main/itemdb.lua`, TOC or addon Lua until a separate production migration is approved.
6. **TRIAGE UNCERTAINTY:** Create a per-field, per-recipe research backlog and update master-owned `research/coordination/assignments.json`. Score tasks by (a) estimated chance of changing the correct liquidation decision or expected gold error, (b) missing mapping or eligibility risk, (c) unskilled-baseline uncertainty and sensitivity, (d) low confidence in a frequently used effect/transform that would help many items, (e) magnitude and recency of source evidence, (f) available independent corroboration, and (g) likelihood/cost of closing the gap. Do not treat the lowest numeric score as automatically the most valuable task; do not conflate missing probability with missing expectedQuantity. Disjointly delegate to Midnight, TWW, Dragonflight and Legacy workers, including cross-expansion reallocation when needed. Assign both precise output IDs/field and precise mechanics hypothesis with source/conditions, rather than generic "raise confidence".
7. **QA, PUBLISH RESEARCH SCORECARD:** Record accepted/rejected fields, changed model candidates, first-pass scope vs actual usable baseline coverage, confidence by each of four fields and expansion, context-known versus unknown source contributions, independently supported historical mechanics rules, verified numerical rules, unresolved patch bridges and prioritized assignments. Record failed/incomplete validations. Preserve append-only audit records and commit hash; avoid noisy re-writes on no new evidence.

## Contextual sample-dating adjudication

For every `TEMPORAL_ATTRIBUTION` work item, the expansion worker submits auditable evidence about actual cast timing—not merely a document publication timestamp. Master QA independently inspects the primary source and distinguishes explicit post/pre mechanic claims, verified source logs/builds, circumstantial "testing the new rates" language, and contradictory historical signs. Master must publish its decision separately as `CONTEXTUALLY_BEFORE/AFTER`, a provisional `LIKELY` alternative, or unresolved, with source claim IDs and rule revision; do not fabricate a cast timestamp. When a single report has pre/post groups, keep cohorts disjoint where supported. Do not add a numerical hotfix multiplier based on timing evidence alone. On mechanics updates replay all unchanged original study evidence with reviewed temporal attributions and record any resulting changes in target itemdb candidate confidence.

## Mandatory reverse / forward model consistency and independent prediction QA (2026-10-28)

After producing a per-item unskilled candidate, **run it forward through historically applicable and independently supported profession mechanics** for each original study context (its skill, node ranks, gear, game patch/quality and recipe). Then compare predicted to observed `dropChance`, `expectedQuantity`, and positive conditional `yieldMin`/`yieldMax` only where direct cohort evidence supports each field. Perform this both as a source-context consistency check and on held-out original studies that were NOT used for estimation, parameter fitting, or tuning. Hold out entire primary studies/datasets (including derivative reposts) to avoid leakage. Source-specific train/validation splits must be recorded in candidate manifests.

Apply grouping/partial pooling, pattern discovery, regression and Bayesian parameter/posterior-predictive methods to select viable mechanic hypotheses; do not mistake in-sample reconstruction or a shared model prior for independent verification. The observational measurement uncertainty, inferred unskilled-baseline confidence, transformation confidence, patch applicability and independent-prediction performance are separate evidence dimensions. Probabilities need binomial or appropriately justified beta-binomial diagnostics; mean E uses actual cast denominators, and a variance-based interval only when a zero-inclusive distribution is known; observed min/max are sample extrema, not automatically hard game bounds. Zero baseline can only acquire positive output through a separately supported additive/unlock/proc/eligibility mechanism, never `0 * multiplier`. Match all four cell values for the same item/output/recipe/patch context and test `p * positiveConditionalMean = E` where that conditional mean is supported.

Master must record systematic prediction residuals and classify failures as: baseline bias, skill/gear/spec model misspecification, missing mechanic, wrong patch epoch, source identity/rank/denominator mismatch, unrecoverable missing context, or insufficient independent study. Refit affected candidates when supported; reassign concrete discrepancies to workers. Confidence may rise with genuinely out-of-sample support, not just with a better fit to training data. Keep below-90 provisional candidates available and clearly labelled.

The open `review/profession-mechanics-registry-v0.2` PR #3 contains a research-only `research/mechanics/BASELINE_FORWARD_VALIDATION.md` protocol, `research/tools/validate_forward_predictions.js` checker and synthetic `research/tests/test_forward_validation.js` regressions. These are **not yet merged** into the hourly master research branch; they check externally supplied predictions and do NOT currently fit regression/Bayesian models, implement historical coefficients, or automatically compile `itemdb.lua`. Treat their absence from the active branch as an engineering dependency, not a validated result.

## Stable assignment classes

- `MAPPING_ELIGIBILITY_GAP`: Missing/uncertain genuine output or recipe/rank eligibility.
- `BASELINE_FIELD_GAP`: One of four baseline fields lacks evidence or is ambiguous.
- `SKILL_BONUS_MECHANIC`: Missing or conflicting modifier, additive/threshold/unlock, or exact relevant stat mapping.
- `HISTORICAL_PATCH_BRIDGE`: Confounded pre/post mechanic era or missing numerical patch effect.
- `TEMPORAL_ATTRIBUTION`: Original source wording/log/build can distinguish the actual observation era; retain alternatives.
- `SOURCE_DENOMINATOR`: Actual cast count, input saved/refunded, or zero-inclusive output totals unresolved.
- `CORROBORATION_CONFLICT`: Independent matched studies disagree substantially, require bias/context audit.
- `MODEL_VALIDATION`: Extrapolation, reverse-to-unskilled estimate, confidence or held-out test requires better evidence.

Each assignment should include `stableKey`, `field` or `mechanicsRuleID`, `method`, `expansionID`, `targetPatch`, `requestedEvidence`, `researchPriorityReason`, `sourceIDsAlreadySeen`, `previousQAFeedback`, `assignedWorker`, `queueRevision` and `expectedDecisionImpact` if grounded. Workers must consult the source-memory registry so they do not repeat already exhausted searches.

## Field and model confidence

Evidence-confidence 0–100 applies to database cells, not source prestige. Preserve measurement uncertainty separately from context/patch relevance and model/transformation uncertainty. Source samples are presumed genuine absent a particular documented defect. A large sample demonstrates a pattern but exact matching skill/bonus conditions affects target applicability. Individual measured `expectedQuantity` can be useful even if probability and positive min/max are unknown. Confidence below 90 is **not** a reason to leave a supportable database cell blank. However, don't invent missing values, guaranteed min/max or effect factors, or call inferred outputs measured.

## Feedback example (illustrative only)

Suppose expected gem quantity is provisional with confidence 50 while drop chance is unknown and the current patch added a documented chance modifier with no amount. Master preserves the provisional expected yield, assigns a matched post-hotfix cohort with zero-inclusive cast records to the appropriate expansion worker, requests corroborating patch-mechanics documentation, then reevaluates both fields and any dependent items after QA. It does **not** block all itemdb candidate work or manufacture an additive coefficient.
