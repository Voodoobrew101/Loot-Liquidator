# Evidence synthesis and inference protocol (v1.1; 2026-10-28)

**Required for the master QA agent and all four expansion workers on EVERY run.** Research branch only. This protocol augments the itemdb and numerical-mechanics contracts; it does not override production release safety. All original sources remain immutable.

## Goal: reuse findings without confusing consensus with proof of current mechanics

For every assigned input/output/method, search existing `research/evidence/observations/*.jsonl`, source ledgers, outside primary studies, community experiments and patch records BEFORE requesting any new in-game testing. A strong independently replicated historical baseline is useful research evidence, even if unrelated output fields lack verification. Distinguish: DIRECT_MEASURED; INDEPENDENTLY_CORROBORATED; HISTORICAL_CONTEXT_INCOMPLETE; PATCH_BRIDGED; MODEL_INFERRED; CONTRADICTORY; UNKNOWN. Do not require dropChance and conditional min/max to be verified before considering a valid **expectedQuantity** baseline. Still show the record-wide minimum confidence separately, and do not claim the full four-field >=90 gate passed.

## Sample-first interpretation and contextual weighting (user direction, 2026-10-28)

**Do not rate a source's prestige and impose that rating as an evidence-confidence ceiling.** Assume an independently identifiable report describes genuine observations unless there is a concrete, recorded reason to dispute it: impossible arithmetic, reversed item IDs, ambiguous units or cast denominator, copied dataset, confirmed manipulation, incompatible game mode or version, or explicit contradictory logs. Distinguish `OBSERVATION_UNCHALLENGED` (default), `OBSERVATION_NEEDS_CLARIFICATION`, `OBSERVATION_CORRECTED`, `DUPLICATE_DATASET`, and `OBSERVATION_INVALID`. A missing skill, stat or patch is an *unmeasured covariate*, not evidence that an author or their observed output totals are unreliable. Preserve raw reports even when they cannot yet be applied to the current Retail configuration.

**Confidence ratings belong to the resulting database fields and their applicability, not to the source as a whole.** Independently record (a) provenance/independence and concrete quality flags; (b) count of actual completed operations (not input quantity masquerading as casts), outcome totals, hit counts or rounded reported means; (c) exact target-context similarity; (d) the estimated `expectedQuantity`, `dropChance` and conditional observed ranges, each with its *own* evidence-based confidence and uncertainty. Do not use the weakest of four fields to suppress useful expected-yield evidence. Record-wide minimum confidence remains a diagnostic, not a reason to discard provisional per-field data.

**Sample magnitude establishes statistical patterns.** Compare independent cohort means and distributions, including those with unknown skill/gear/patch as clearly labelled *exploratory* strata. For truly homogeneous independent cohorts with valid denominators, pool unconditional means as `sum(totalUnits)/sum(actualOperations)`: more actual operations contribute proportionally more evidence. A source-reported mean with a verified operation count but no exact raw total may inform a separately labelled rounded-mean synthesis without fabricating an integer total or standard error. Compare heterogeneity, uncertainty and independent studies rather than requiring every source to disclose all conditions before seeing recurring patterns. If operation-level variability is missing, do not invent narrow confidence intervals.

**Weight target relevance by known profession configuration, separate from sample size.** First stratify by exact item/recipe/batch, game mode, input/output rank, and actual or compatible patch. For a target profile, rank contexts: `EXACT_MATCH` (skill, specialization, equipment, known bonuses, patch), `MATCH_KNOWN_CONDITIONS`, `PARTIAL_MATCH`, `CONTEXT_UNKNOWN`, and `KNOWN_INCOMPATIBLE`. Within a compatible stratum, actual sample counts or measured precision govern statistical weight. Across strata, use explicit, validated profession-effects or similarity models; precise matched-context cohorts should dominate target-specific estimates over huge but mismatched ones. *Do not invent fixed universal numerical context weights, skill slopes, or patch coefficients*, and never describe a context-weighted model as a directly pooled observation. Record the exact known/missing covariates and model assumptions.

**Default output of research:** produce the best supported, context-labelled historical/reference baseline and a separate provisional target-context recommendation when defensible, regardless of whether four fields reach 90. The database may use visibly provisional historical/model-based fallbacks for economic advice under the user's explicit below-90 allowance; do not silently relabel them verified current-Retail rates or apply an unsupported hotfix/spec multiplier. Leave an unobserved probability or conditional min/max unknown unless supported by a separately labelled model. Never invent an eligible item/output relationship.

**Independence is per primary *dataset/cohort*, not per URL.** Different original commenters can provide independent experiments on the same Wowhead page; one author may provide several distinct cohorts from the same study. Preserve each non-overlapping cohort, but do not count multiple posts, mirrors, or revisions of the same underlying observations as independent. Source review uses studyID, original author, cohortID and dataset ancestry/fingerprint where available, and unresolved independence stays flagged.

**Report distinct measures:** counts of studies accepted as observational evidence, exact/partially/unknown-context cohorts, total independently reported actual operations, candidate baseline coverage and model/provisional coverage, each field's DB confidence, and high-confidence current-applicable rules. First-pass source audits and all-four-fields >=90 are not substitutes for reporting useful sample-derived coverage.


## Historical-mechanics replay and observation-time provenance (2026-10-28)

Research observations are **immutable evidence**, not permanently normalized-to-current calculations. Preserve the raw published totals, actual completed casts, output histograms, source publication metadata, reported observation date/range, game mode, client build/hotfix, exact item/recipe/rank, and known profession conditions. Do not rewrite raw output numbers as the ProfessionMechanicsRegistry evolves. A correction is a linked audit/revision, not silent mutation.

Record optional `observationTiming` fields defined in `research/evidence/observation.schema.json`. Prioritize (a) actual cast timestamp or date range, then (b) source-reported experiment date, then (c) **original source publication date only as an upper bound** on when casts could have happened. Retrieval date is *never* a substitute for experiment date. Never invent a day from an undated, month-only, year-only or republished source. A sample published after a hotfix may contain pre-hotfix measurements. Preserve known/unknown provenance explicitly.

At every proposed `itemdb.lua` rebuild, re-read **all** immutable evidence under `research/evidence/observations/` and apply the latest **reviewed, versioned historical mechanics timeline** to each sample's **actual observation era**, not today's rules. Mechanic event effective date/build and the registry's own refinement/review date are different clocks. An updated historical event/coefficients may reclassify unchanged old studies; preserve both revision history and source-cohort provenance. Partition incompatible eras before fitting unskilled baselines; infer cross-era transforms only with separately supported numerical evidence. Recompute affected four-field confidence estimates and candidate records after registry revisions; record evidence-file checksums, registry/timeline versions and model parameters for reproducibility.

Prototype temporal classification and replay scripts are staged on the open `review/profession-mechanics-registry-v0.2` branch. Their existence does not imply they have been merged into the hourly agent's active research branch or validated live in WoW. Until integrated, agents must collect and annotate timing information in immutable observations and report contradictions. Remain research-only; no production release without review.
## Contextual dating of original studies (mandatory; 2026-10-28)

When actual cast date/build is not stated, the assigned worker must investigate the original source before classifying the study's mechanics epoch. Collect exact citations and statements (and original author/comment context where available) from the experiment, publication, edit history, screenshots, in-game tooltip/skill-tree terminology, linked raw logs, and discussion replies. A study soon after a hotfix is often an experiment *about* the change: this motivates targeted examination but is not proof that its casts happened after the change. Treat original publication day as an upper bound; the page retrieval day is never the cast day.

Record a revisionable, event-specific **temporal attribution** with exact claim excerpts, source URL, event ID, evidence type, competing plausible eras, and proposed conclusion: BEFORE, AFTER, LIKELY_BEFORE, LIKELY_AFTER, AMBIGUOUS or CONTRADICTORY. If the author explicitly states casts occurred after the named hotfix, or source-linked logs/build prove the epoch, flag a potentially strong event-relative placement for independent master review even when an exact calendar cast day is missing. Wording like "testing new rates" supports a provisional *likely* hypothesis at most, not a verified cast date. If a study combines before/after cohorts, split only when original data permits. An assignment with unresolved dating must report exactly which original context clues were examined and why alternative eras remain possible.

Research-only `research/evidence/temporal-attribution.schema.json` is available to record this separately from immutable raw cast results. The open review branch `review/profession-mechanics-registry-v0.2` stages a contextual-date classifier/replay adapter; do not assume it is merged or pass a worker's proposed interpretation as master-verified. Chronology assignments must remain repeatable when the master refines historical mechanics later.

## Forward-model predictive corroboration (2026-10-28)

Independent replication must test more than an observed sample mean. For each candidate neutral-baseline/profession-mechanic hypothesis, calculate predicted four-field outcomes at the ORIGINAL study's patch, recipe/rank and reported skill/spec/gear/bonus profile. Preserve original cast counts and distributions; do not force missing context or probability from mean, and do not use histograms or output values from the holdout cohort to tune the prediction first. Use study/dataset-family holdout to prevent correlated copies from inflating corroboration. Explain how the observed results compare to the model's measurement uncertainty and inferred mechanic uncertainty; field confidence may rise with independent accurate prediction, not circular agreement with its own training data. For an unexplained mismatch, workers should investigate actual source context, version changes and independent studies before arguing any model coefficient must change. A qualitative patch note defines mechanic/epoch behavior but not its numeric slope. The PR #3 review branch contains research-only forward-validation specifications, not a live verified numeric model.

## Worker process

1. **Recover existing data:** Index sources by exact input ID and quality, output ID and quality, recipe ID, batch size, game mode, expansion, patch/build, skill, spec, equipment, stats. Find published zero-inclusive operation-level data or original total+actual operation count, not just a derived TSM table. Revisit already reviewed studies to extract field-specific quantities. Report the number of *new* studies and *reused* studies separately; never double-count a review as a distinct experiment.
2. **Cohort ancestry and independence:** Capture unique observationID, studyID, dataset fingerprint, original URL, author/experiment identity when disclosed, actual cohort ID, derived/copy relationships, and publication date. Twenty links repeating the same spreadsheet are **one** experiment. Distinct authors' experiments on the same forum or Wowhead page may be independent when the primary reports support that; do not deduplicate by page URL alone. Flag unresolved ancestry, overlapping/reposted measurements and dataset revisions; do not multiply sample sizes.
3. **Compatibility:** Directly pool only genuinely matching game mode, recipe, input/output identity and quality, batch, patch or explicitly compatible patch epoch, and relevant skill/spec/gear/stat conditions. Independently examine patterns within unknown-condition cohorts as historical/exploratory evidence; do not treat unknown as proof of mismatch or match. Retain separate observed study means and context-similarity rankings; do not auto-label exploratory cohorts current-Retail.
4. **Field-level extraction:** Calculate E_i = sum(output_i units)/actual completed operations including zeros, if verified denominator; report the denominator's provenance/quality. Calculate p_i = hit operations / actual operations ONLY when hit counts or zero-inclusive histograms exist. Conditional observed positive min/max come only from per-operation positive yields; unknown remains null. Never value with yield midpoint or multiply E by p a second time. Input Resourcefulness savings, refund quantities, output rank transitions and operation time are independent effects.
5. **Synthesis:** For compatible, independent cohorts, weight by actual completed-operation count (or appropriate measured precision), not by subjective source-prestige scores, only when denominators are genuinely equivalent and non-overlapping. Preserve per-cohort estimates, total operations, confidence, source URLs, and disagreement. For sparse/rare outcomes prefer Wilson/appropriate exact intervals for p; for E compute a sampling interval only if operation-level variance is known. Without variance, report the range of cohort means and explicit 'uncertainty unknown', NOT a fabricated 95% CI. Compare independent studies, include contradictory findings, detect outliers and possible selection/reporting bias, and identify held-out sources.
6. **Patch reasoning:** Build a chronological source-backed event table. A documented exact +X% change can yield a *candidate* patch-specific transform only if it applies to the exact recipe, output and condition and defines what quantity changed. Qualitative notes permit **direction only**, never a numeric multiplier. A pre/post measured ratio requires independently sourced comparable cohorts, actual denominators, uncertainty and confounder audit. Never assume absent patch notes means no changes. Save raw baseline and transform with independent provenance; don't mutate historical observations.
7. **Inference:** Estimate an unmeasured output/rank/skill cohort only when a validated applicable relationship and all required inputs exist. Label MODEL_INFERRED, preserve formula, parameters, dependency IDs, conditions, uncertainty propagation, sensitivity checks and holdout results. No extrapolation across expansions, ranks, skills, recipes or hotfixes without explicitly tested bridge. An inferred output cannot establish its own missing eligibility.
8. **Promotion:** Workers propose separate field-level promotion candidates (not production writes): evidence type, value, uncertainty, source IDs, independence grouping, patch/context scope, confidence 0–100 and original QA blockers. For gold EV, propose `expectedQuantity` alone where well-supported even when `dropChance`/`yieldMin`/`yieldMax` are null. A fully current-applicable claim still requires review and matching conditions, but a clearly labelled historical/modelled provisional fallback may be staged for the user's below-90 database coverage goal. Never silently present an unknown-context baseline as a verified current-Retail measurement. The master independently audits arithmetic, ancestors, statistical quality, applicability and holdout discrepancies before any candidate approval. Master may mark a field as corroborated without marking whole record >=90. Don't numerically inflate evidence scores solely because the source count is large.
9. **Prioritize next measurements:** Rank missing experiments by expected improvement in the *actual* liquidation decision per marginal gold/time, not by which keys are easiest to count. Prefer small paired pre/post or low/high controlled experiments that disambiguate competing models. Record expected net testing cost and probability of changing the recommended action. Don't mandate 300 casts of every material.

## Machine-readable synthesis

Use `node research/tools/synthesize_studies.js research/evidence/observations/*.jsonl` on a checkout for conservative, **research-only** grouped candidates. The tool:
- counts source independence conservatively by studyID and primary source link; reposts are not independent;
- only produces unconditional means when completed operations are explicitly recorded and not flagged unknown; does not substitute 5-ore equivalents for real operations;
- partitions by exact patch/build and prof context, flags missing context, and never autogenerates patch transforms or current Retail status;
- reports source counts, conflicts and blockers rather than treating all consistent text reports as 90+ evidence.

The machine result is a **starting point**: humans/agents must resolve duplicate datasets, confirm denominators and compare contexts. No direct codegen, no forced >=90 gate bypass.

## Master QA outputs each run

Include: new and reused primary studies; independent versus repeated sources; field-level direct/corroborated/inferred/historical-only candidates; disagreements and statistical caveats; patch bridges validated versus blocked; measured per-field confidence changes; gold-cost avoided by usable external data (only if quantified); remaining targeted in-game experiments; and unchanged current compiled/released state. Preserve first-pass item coverage and four-field 90 score tallies as **separate** metrics from newly synthesizable expectedQuantity values.

Example: historical Titanium 1,000-ore study and TSM totals within about 1% support a *historical input-normalized corroboration*. The report did not establish actual completed cast count or current patch, so `0.52 per derived five-ore equivalent` is NOT a verified per-operation current Retail expectedQuantity. The TSM rates may not be independent of community data until their origin is determined.
