## What should I do with this item?

Loot Liquidator puts a small letter on every item in your **bags, bank, Warband bank, guild bank and BetterBags** telling you the best thing to do with it, and the tooltip shows the gold values behind that advice.

It only advises. It never sells, mails, crafts or destroys anything.

### The letters

- **A** – sell on the Auction House
- **V** – sell to a vendor
- **D** – disenchant
- **M / P / S** – mill, prospect or smelt
- **C / R / X** – craft, recycle, or exchange/combine/split
- **T** – keep it: you or an alt is missing its appearance
- **?** – not enough information (rare)

### A clean tooltip

One header line with the answer and its value, e.g. **"Loot Liquidator: Smelt 6g 87s"**. Pick how much more you want to see: *Off*, *Recommendation*, *Alternatives* (every option with its gold value) or *Breakdown* (the materials behind each option, with drop chance and amount).

Disenchant, mill, prospect and smelt values show a **confidence** percentage, so you know how solid each number is.

### Gets smarter as you play

- Uses your **profession skill**, or an alt's, or a "what-if" profile you set up.
- Counts your **alts** for transmog and processing, but only for items you can actually send them.
- Can **record your own** disenchant/mill/prospect/smelt results, so the numbers become yours. Export them to share with friends, and import theirs.

### Requirements

- **TradeSkillMaster** for Auction House prices (without it, only vendor values are compared).
- Optional: **BetterBags**, **AllTheThings** (helps recognise gear tokens for now).
- Retail World of Warcraft: Midnight.

### Settings

Type **/ll** or open Settings → AddOns → Loot Liquidator. Everything uses Blizzard's own settings controls.

*This is a test release: please report anything odd with the item name, the letter you saw and what you expected.*
